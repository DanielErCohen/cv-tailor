## Keywords

- **Matched from the posting:** onboarding, retention, net revenue retention, quarterly business reviews, time to value, repeatable, usage data (via "usage-based risk list"), Salesforce, SQL, mid-market (via the summary framing; the user's book is mid-size logistics firms).
- **In the posting, left out for lack of evidence:** retail and e-commerce experience, enterprise accounts above GBP 250k ARR.

## Gaps

- **Retail or e-commerce:** a plus in the posting, no evidence in the CV. Do not add. The cover note states the gap in one honest line and points to the closest real experience, operations-heavy logistics customers.
- **Accounts above GBP 250k ARR:** the Fernhill book averages about GBP 52k per account (GBP 3.1M over 60). Do not claim enterprise experience. If any single account exceeded GBP 250k, add it to the achievements bank first.
