# Job description

- **Role:** Customer Success Manager, Mid-Market
- **Company:** Northgate Analytics
- **Link:** https://example.com/careers/customer-success-manager

- **Location and type:** Leeds or remote in the UK, full-time

## Posting

Northgate Analytics sells reporting and forecasting software to mid-size retailers. We are growing our mid-market team and need a Customer Success Manager to own a book of 50 to 70 accounts.

You will:
- Own onboarding, adoption and renewal for mid-market retail accounts.
- Run quarterly business reviews with customer finance and operations leaders.
- Spot churn risk early using product usage data and act on it.
- Work with product and sales to bring customer needs into the roadmap.
- Help us build a repeatable onboarding programme as we scale.

You have:
- 4 or more years in customer success or account management for B2B software.
- A record of improving retention or net revenue retention.
- Comfort with data: you can pull your own usage reports, SQL is a plus.
- Experience with Salesforce and a customer success platform.
- Excellent written communication.
- Experience in retail or e-commerce is a plus.
- Experience managing enterprise accounts above GBP 250k ARR is a plus.
