# 04 Tailor to a company

Two postings with similar titles can want different things. Fifteen minutes of research tells you which of your achievements to lead with and gives your cover note a specific opening.

## Steps

1. In the application folder, open `company-notes.md`.
2. Say:
   > Use company-research for [company], role [role]. Here is what I found: [paste the About page, a recent news item, anything useful].
   If your Claude can browse the web, it will look itself and tell you what it could not check.
3. Check the sources. Every fact should be marked Verified (with a link and date) or Unverified.
4. Read the "Use in CV" and "Use in cover note" sections. They say which two or three achievements go first and what the opening line of the cover note can mention.
5. Run `cv-tailor` (guide 03), then `cover-note`:
   > Use cover-note for applications/2026-03-02-acme-analyst.
6. Check the cover note: 120 to 180 words, every claim in your CV, one specific reason for this company, no clichés.

## What to learn about a company

- What they sell and to whom.
- What changed recently: funding, launches, leaders, markets.
- The words they use for their own work.
- Why this role exists now.
- What they say about how they work.
- Questions you want answered in an interview.

## What research must never do

Research shapes emphasis and tone. Claim only skills you have. Praise only what you admire.

## Good first lines for a cover note

Names the role, then one specific thing about the company that connects to your work. Example: "I am applying for the Customer Success Manager role at Northgate Analytics. Your move into mid-size retail last quarter matches the account onboarding work I led at Fernhill Software." The example folder has a full version.
