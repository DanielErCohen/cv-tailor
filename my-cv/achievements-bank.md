## Prompts to jog your memory

- Revenue won, saved or protected: how much, over what period?
- Time saved: from how long to how long?
- Volume handled: customers, tickets, deals, users, items per week.
- Quality: error rate, satisfaction score, ratings, audit results.
- Growth: percent change, from what to what.
- Scale: team size, budget, number of markets, number of stakeholders.
- Firsts: first to do something at the company, something you built from nothing.
- Recognition: awards, promotions, requests to repeat the work elsewhere.
